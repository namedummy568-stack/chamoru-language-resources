# CHamoru Grammar Rules

1.  **Word Order**: Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) is common, but Verb-Subject-Object (VSO) is also frequently used, especially in declarative sentences.
2.  **Verb Conjugation**: CHamoru verbs are conjugated to indicate tense, aspect, mood, and the person and number of the subject and object. This often involves prefixes, suffixes, and infixes.
